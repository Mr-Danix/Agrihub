AgriHub

The Digital Harvest

AgriHub isn't just a marketplace; it's a social revolution designed for the Ethiopian agricultural landscape. By digitizing the supply chain, we connect farmers in Gojjam, Sidama, and Oromia directly to urban centers like Addis Ababa.

Our goal is to eliminate the 'information asymmetry' that often leaves farmers underpaid. Through AgriHub, every kilo of Premium Teff or Sidama Coffee sold carries the value of the hard work put into the soil.
